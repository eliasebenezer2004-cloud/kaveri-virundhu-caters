Traditional Ila Virundhu

The standard setup for a Trichy wedding. Guests sit in rows on the floor (or on chairs — your call), and our team serves the 21 items one row at a time. We bring 4 servers per row so it doesn’t drag.

  • Authentic floor mat seating or traditional table setups.
  • Dedicated supervisor for every 30 guests.
  • Staff dressed in traditional Dhoti/Saree uniform.
❖ நவீன விருந்து ❖ Modern Elegance

Round-Table Buffet & Live Counters

For receptions, engagement days, and company events. We bring the chafing dishes, the gas stoves, and a guy who does a fruit carving while your guests watch. No rental company needed.

  • VIP Round-table service for distinguished guests.
  • Live Dosa, Chaat, and Barbeque stations.
  • We bring our own chafing dishes — you don’t rent anything.
