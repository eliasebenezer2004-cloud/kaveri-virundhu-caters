Before You Call — Questions People Ask

Do you bring tables and chairs?
We bring the food, serving vessels, and staff. Tables, chairs, and mandapam you arrange — or we can refer our decorator.
What’s the advance amount?
&inr;5,000 to block the date, 30% one week before, balance on the day.
Can you do Jain food?
Yes — no onion, no garlic, no carrot, no beetroot. Tell us when you call.
How far do you travel from Trichy?
Anywhere within 60 km. Beyond that, &inr;25/km extra for the vehicle.
What about leftover food?
We pack it in foil containers for you to distribute. No charge.
Is the tasting free?
Yes, if your event is 200+ guests. Otherwise &inr;200/person, adjusted against the final bill.
